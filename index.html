Travel Tips for Foreign Visitors

🚆

Subway Lines & T-Money

Michuhol-gu is conveniently served by Seoul Subway Line 1 (Juan, Jemulpo, Dowon) and Incheon Line 2. Use a T-Money or Climate Card for seamless transfers!

🪙

Brass Coin Market Experience

At Sinki Market, exchange KRW for historical Yeopjeon (traditional brass coins) at the customer center to purchase food items around the market like a Joseon merchant!

☎️

1330 Korea Travel Hotline

Call 1330 (available in English, Chinese, Japanese, Russian) for 24/7 free translation assistance, direction help, or emergency support anywhere in Incheon.

🗺️

Navigation App Tip

Google Maps has limited walking directions in Korea. We recommend downloading Naver Map or KakaoMap (both support English UI) for precise routes.
